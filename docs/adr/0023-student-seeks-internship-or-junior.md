# The surface states internship or junior

The CV is the positioning evidence. It names the Owner as a backend developer and a student of Systems Analysis and Development at FATEC Ipiranga, in progress since February 2026. Index, Profile, and Resume use that title and that target: an internship or a junior backend role. They do not sell Backend Engineer.

This supersedes the ban on the word júnior in ADR 0013. A count of years of professional experience stays off the surface. The Employer named in the CV stays off the surface; see ADR 0007. The course and the school are not an Employer.
